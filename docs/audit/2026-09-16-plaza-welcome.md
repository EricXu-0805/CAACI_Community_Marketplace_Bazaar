Welcome to Illini Market 👋 欢迎来到 Illini 集市

Moving in, moving out, or just making room? List what you no longer need, find something nearby, or post a Wanted request and let sellers find you.

A quick start:
• Home → On sale or Wanted → Post. Add a clear photo, price or budget, condition, and a pickup area. Mention scratches, missing parts and other details you'd want to know as a buyer.
• Open a listing or seller's profile to start a chat. Use Messages to keep track of the conversation and arrange pickup.
• Plaza is for campus updates, useful tips and everyday discoveries. Add photos and link your own listing when it helps.
• Switch 中 / EN for the interface. Keep important details clear in either language.

A few shared rules: be honest, be kind, and keep it relevant. No scams, harassment, illegal goods or academic cheating services. Use Report when something seems wrong. Meet in a public place, inspect the item, and avoid paying a stranger in advance. Campus pickup labels describe a meeting area; they don't guarantee a person's identity or an item's condition.

We'll share practical tips, community news and product updates here. If something feels confusing or breaks, use Feedback in your profile and tell us the page and device. Thanks for helping shape this place.
